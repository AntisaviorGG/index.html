How It Works

Three Steps to Your Shirt

No minimums. No inventory. No hassle. Just your design on a shirt, delivered to your door.

1

Design It

Use the TeeCraft app to design on your phone, or upload your own artwork. Text, images, shapes — whatever your team needs.

2

We Print It

Your shirt is printed on demand by our production partner and quality-checked before it ships. No waste, no warehouses.

3

Wear It & Win

Your shirt arrives in 5–9 days (US) or up to 25 days (international). Wear it proud — you're on the team now.

The Corny Crew

More Than a Shirt Store. A Team.

When you buy a Corny Inc. shirt or design one on TeeCraft, you're not just a customer — you're on the team. The Corny Crew is a community of people who design, wear, and celebrate together.

  • Design of the Week — Submit your design, the community votes, the winner gets their shirt produced and a free one for themselves.
  • First 100 Founding Members — The first 100 buyers get a permanent 10% discount, a digital badge, and their name on the Founders Wall.
  • Monthly Design Contests — Win $50 cash plus a 10% revenue share on your winning design for 6 months.
  • Customer Spotlights — Every Friday, we feature a real Crew member and their story.
  • Refer a Friend — Bring someone onto the team and you both get 15% off.

Our Story

Why Corny Inc.?

People don't just buy shirts. They buy belonging. They buy the feeling of being part of something — a squad, a crew, a family, a team.

Corny Inc. started with a simple idea: every person deserves a team, and every team deserves a shirt. Whether you're celebrating a championship, a birthday, a graduation, or just a Tuesday with your favorite people — your shirt should say "I belong here."

We print on demand, which means no waste and no warehouses full of shirts nobody wants. Your shirt is made when you order it, just for you.
